This page has been created to gather suggestions and feedback regarding our OSPF course for CCNA candidates. It is intentionally made public so that everyone can read and participate. Everything must be transparent for us all.

## We Value Your Input!

Thank you for participating in our OSPF (Open Shortest Path First) course. **Your experience and insights are essential for us to improve and continually deliver high-quality content**. Whether you’ve found certain aspects of the course valuable or encountered areas where we can enhance, we would love to hear from you!

Please use the comment section below to share your suggestions, feedback, or any ideas that you believe could help make this course even better.

## Wondering What Can You Share Feedback On?

I'll give a few ideas on what topics you can share feedback or make suggestions. However, don't limit yourself to these areas of the course; instead, share your honest thoughts.

- Course Content
  - Did the lessons effectively cover OSPF fundamentals required by the CCNA curriculum?
  - Should we include additional OSPF topics?
  - Were there any topics that needed more detail or better clarity?
- Practical Labs & Exercises
  - Were the labs and exercises helpful in reinforcing the OSPF concepts?
  - Could there be improvements in the structure or type of hands-on tasks?
- Course Delivery
  - How was the pacing and delivery of the material?
  - Is the website fast and responsive? Were the quality of the images good on your device screen?
- Additional Resources
  - Are there additional resources (such as reading materials, reference links, or tools) that would enhance your understanding of OSPF?
- Other Suggestions
  - Do you have any other thoughts on improving the overall course?
  - We’re open to any ideas that would help fellow students!

## Do you want to help us grow?

If the course helped you along your journey toward the CCNA certification and you would like to help us grow, you can do the following:

- Share the course on any of the social platforms you use.
- Tell friends that study for the CCNA exam.
- Like us on Facebook, Linkedin, and all other social media.

Thank you!
